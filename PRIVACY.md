# 🔒 Habbify Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
Habbify collects minimal personal information solely to provide app services and user authentication:
- **Account Details:** Name, email address, username, and profile picture (via Google Sign-In / Firebase Auth).
- **Productivity & Health Data:** Habits, routines, goals, workouts, financial logs, and journal entries (stored locally on your device and synced privately to your account).
- **Social Features:** User handle, friend connections, and voluntarily shared achievements.

## 2. How We Use Information
Your data is used strictly for:
- User authentication and multi-device data synchronization.
- Displaying personal statistics, level progress, and friend leaderboards.

## 3. Data Protection & Sharing
- **No Third-Party Ads or Trackers:** We do not use advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or sell user data.
- **Data Ownership:** You own your data. You can delete or purge all account data anytime via **Settings ➔ Danger Zone ➔ Purge All Data**.

## 4. Contact Us
For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us via GitHub: [github.com/Dreamer5967/habbify-windows](https://github.com/Dreamer5967/habbify-windows).
